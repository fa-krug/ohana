# What is Ohana?

Ohana is one app for the whole family: a shared **calendar**, the family's **finances**,
**meals** (recipes, meal plan, shopping in Apple Reminders), **chores**, **doctor appointments** and an
**AI assistant**
that works across all of them.

It is open source and **self-hosted**. You run the server (one Docker image, one SQLite file) at
home or on any small machine, and the family uses the **native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac**.
Nobody else holds your family's data: not us, not a cloud service you didn't choose.

Ready to try it? Go to the [quick start](/quickstart).

## Families and people

- Several **families** per account. Members are **editors** (change everything) or **viewers**
  (only look).
- **Invite links** create an account and add it to the family in one go. An administrator can
  also create accounts with a one-time **setup link**.
- Members **without an account** (children, grandparents) with birthdays: they show up in the
  calendar, when booking an appointment ("who is it for?") and in what the assistant knows.
- Everyone has a **color** and, if you like, a **photo** (cropped as you choose), so you see at a
  glance whose day, entry or chore it is.
- Sign in with **passkeys** or a password.

## Calendar

- The family's **year at a glance**: vacation, public holidays, sick days, day-care closures.
- Entry types per year with roles, so you see the **days nobody covers**. Each type belongs to a
  family member and shows in their color, with an icon (suggested from its name).
- Notes, **events** over one day or several, repeating like in Apple Calendar (e.g. a booked
  doctor's appointment, swimming every Thursday) and **birthdays**; on iPad and Mac an event is
  dragged onto another day to move it.
- A **two-way sync with Apple Calendar** on your devices (iCloud, Google or Exchange calendars).
- Days marked as *away* are skipped by the meal plan.

## Overview and the week ahead

- The **overview** shows today, what's coming up day by day, the meals, birthdays, the month's
  spending and your appointment searches, and asks the assistant from its text field.
- Once a week (Sunday evening, unless the server says otherwise) the **week ahead** checks the next
  two weeks for each person: events that overlap, too little time to get from one place to the
  next, packed days, events while you're away, children who need more drivers than there are,
  birthdays while you're away. Editors can hide what's fine as it is.

## Finance

- Transactions, accounts and a **category tree**, transfers between accounts and **split
  bookings**.
- **CSV import** of bank statements with saved formats per bank, duplicates recognized.
- **Bank sync** of an account or a Visa credit card with **comdirect**: the server fetches the
  booked transactions, you confirm the login with a TAN
  ([how](/app#syncing-an-account-with-its-bank)).
- **Scan a receipt** in the app: amount, date and shop are read on the device, the category
  filled in; keep the scan as a PDF if you like.
- Monthly overviews, comparisons and a benchmark; every number opens the transactions behind it,
  a category its payees.
- **Insights**: which categories grew against your usual month and which payees caused it,
  **regular payments** (subscriptions, price changes, ones that stopped), small amounts that add
  up, and what looks wrong in the data: likely duplicates, transfers missing their other leg,
  unusual amounts or categories. Editors mark what's fine as such.
- Categorizing with the assistant.

## Meals

- **Recipes** with ingredients, steps (including kitchen-machine steps), photos and tags.
- A **meal plan** that picks recipes sharing ingredients and skips days the family is away; on
  iPad and Mac you drag a recipe onto a day, or one day onto another to swap them.
- What the meal plan needs goes onto your **shopping list in Apple Reminders**: pick a list
  (shared over iCloud if you like) and every shopping day's items land there, due on that day.
- A **cook view** for the kitchen: durations in a step start a timer, shown on the Lock Screen
  while it runs.
- Recipes and photos **generated by AI** if you like.
- Recipes **imported from any web page** by the AI: share the page from Safari, use the Shortcut
  or paste its address in the app, and the recipe lands on your *to try* list with its photo.
  Shared from Safari, the import runs on the server and you get a notification when it's there.

## Chores

- **Chores** for anyone in the family, with or without an account: once, due on a day (and a
  time, if you like), or **repeating** (daily, weekly, every second Saturday, …).
- Each chore belongs to a part of the day: **morning, day or evening** (from its time, if it has
  one; the family chooses when each starts). The app shows them that way, day by day, with what's
  overdue.
- Tick a chore off in the app; a repeating chore each time it comes round.
- **Notifications on your devices** when a chore is due, for the chores you choose: the ones you
  add are ticked for you, and anyone in the family with an account can tick the others.

## Appointments

- **Searches** that watch **Doctolib** and **jameda** for free appointments with the doctors you
  pick and **notify** you when one shows up (or only when it's earlier than the last one).
- Booked appointments go into the calendar.
- Doctolib and jameda are German services: if you live elsewhere, turn the module off
  (`DISABLED_MODULES=appointments`).

## Letters

- **Scan a letter** (a school note, a notice, an invitation) with the camera, or share a photo or
  PDF with Ohana. The text is read on the device.
- Save it as a **searchable PDF** into a folder of your choice, with a name ("date_Person_Title")
  and Finder tags suggested from the letter: whose it is, what it's about.
- With the assistant on, its **events, tasks and deadlines** are listed for you to tick: events
  go into the family's calendar, tasks and deadlines into Reminders.

## Assistant

- Chats with any **OpenAI-compatible** model: a cloud provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google,
  OpenRouter, …) or your own (Ollama, LM Studio).
- It can **read and change** the calendar, finances, meals, chores and appointments, and every change
  can be **undone**. Viewers get a read-only assistant.
- It can also read your **reminders** and your **Apple Calendar** and change them; you confirm
  every such change in the app first.
- It finds the events, tasks and deadlines in a **letter** you attach and offers them to add.
- Web search with your own key (Tavily or Brave).
- Answers keep running on the server when you close the app; you can get a notification when
  they're done.

## Notifications

**In the app** on iPhone, iPad and Mac (through fa-krug's push relay, encrypted end to end, so
the relay and Apple can't read them), and to Pushover, Pushbullet, Telegram, email (SMTP) or
**ntfy** (self-hostable push), set per family: new appointments, days without cover, the week
ahead, a finished assistant answer. Chores that are due go to the app only, to whoever ticked
them.

## Languages

English and German, in the app and on the server's pages.

## How it fits together

```
 iPhone / iPad / Mac app ──HTTPS──▶  ohana (server/)            ohana-worker (same image)
   (apple/, SwiftUI)                  API /api/v1               assistant turns, appointment
                                      sign-in pages             searches, notifications, backups
                                            └──── /app/data: SQLite (WAL), photos, backups ────┘
```

- **The server** ([`server/`](../server)) is TypeScript on Node.js: [Hono](https://hono.dev) for
  HTTP, Drizzle ORM over SQLite, passkeys and passwords. It runs as two containers from one
  image: `ohana` serves the API and the sign-in pages, `ohana-worker` does everything that takes
  time (assistant answers, appointment searches, recipe imports, notifications, nightly backups).
- **The app** ([`apple/`](../apple)) is a native SwiftUI app for iOS/iPadOS 26 and macOS 26. It
  keeps no data of its own beyond a copy for offline use: every screen reads and writes the
  server's API with the device's own token. Without a server it shows a read-only demo family.
- **The API** is open and documented ([API](/api)): personal API tokens let your own scripts and
  AI agents use it too.

## Modules you don't need

Every module except the calendar can be turned off per server, e.g.
`DISABLED_MODULES=appointments,finance`. It disappears from the app, its API answers 404, the
worker skips its jobs and the assistant loses its tools. The data stays; removing the setting
brings it back.

## Where it comes from

Ohana replaced three apps written for one family: a vacation calendar with finances and AI
(*family-planner*), a recipe and meal planner (*cookless*) and a doctor's appointment watcher
(*findmyappointment*). [Background and decisions](/plan) tells the story.
