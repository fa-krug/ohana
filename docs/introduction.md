# What is Ohana?

Ohana is one app for the whole family: a shared **calendar**, the family's **finances**,
**meals** (recipes, meal plan, shopping lists), **doctor appointments** and an **AI assistant**
that works across all of them.

It is open source and **self-hosted**. You run the server (one Docker image, one SQLite file) at
home or on any small machine, and the family uses the **native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac**.
Nobody else holds your family's data: not us, not a cloud service you didn't choose.

Ready to try it? Go to the [quick start](/quickstart).

## Families and people

- Several **families** per account. Members are **editors** (change everything) or **viewers**
  (only look).
- **Invite links** create an account and add it to the family in one go. An administrator can
  also create accounts with a one-time **setup link**.
- Members **without an account** (children, grandparents) with birthdays: they show up in the
  calendar, when booking an appointment ("who is it for?") and in what the assistant knows.
- Sign in with **passkeys** or a password.

## Calendar

- The family's **year at a glance**: vacation, public holidays, sick days, day-care closures.
- Entry types per year with roles, so you see the **days nobody covers**.
- Notes, **timed events** (e.g. a booked doctor's appointment) and **birthdays**.
- An **iCalendar feed** for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or any other calendar app.
- Days marked as *away* are skipped by the meal plan.

## Finance

- Transactions, accounts and a **category tree**, transfers between accounts and **split
  bookings**.
- **CSV import** of bank statements with saved formats per bank, duplicates recognized.
- Monthly overviews, comparisons and a benchmark.
- Categorizing with the assistant, and grocery spending matched to your shopping trips.

## Meals

- **Recipes** with ingredients, steps (including kitchen-machine steps), photos and tags.
- A **meal plan** that picks recipes sharing ingredients and skips days the family is away.
- **Shopping lists** grouped by aisle that work **offline** in the supermarket.
- A **cook view** for the kitchen.
- Recipes and photos **generated by AI** if you like.

## Appointments

- **Searches** that watch **Doctolib** and **jameda** for free appointments with the doctors you
  pick and **notify** you when one shows up (or only when it's earlier than the last one).
- Booked appointments go into the calendar.
- Doctolib and jameda are German services: if you live elsewhere, turn the module off
  (`DISABLED_MODULES=appointments`).

## Assistant

- Chats with any **OpenAI-compatible** model: a cloud provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google,
  OpenRouter, …) or your own (Ollama, LM Studio).
- It can **read and change** the calendar, finances, meals and appointments, and every change
  can be **undone**. Viewers get a read-only assistant.
- Web search with your own key (Tavily or Brave).
- Answers keep running on the server when you close the app; you can get a notification when
  they're done.

## Notifications

Pushover, Pushbullet, Telegram, email (SMTP) or **ntfy** (self-hostable push), set per family:
new appointments, days without cover, tomorrow's shopping list, a finished assistant answer.

## Languages

English and German, in the app and on the server's pages.

## How it fits together

```
 iPhone / iPad / Mac app ──HTTPS──▶  ohana (server/)            ohana-worker (same image)
   (apple/, SwiftUI)                  API /api/v1               assistant turns, appointment
                                      sign-in pages             searches, notifications, backups
                                            └──── /app/data: SQLite (WAL), photos, backups ────┘
```

- **The server** ([`server/`](../server)) is TypeScript on Node.js: [Hono](https://hono.dev) for
  HTTP, Drizzle ORM over SQLite, passkeys and passwords. It runs as two containers from one
  image: `ohana` serves the API and the sign-in pages, `ohana-worker` does everything that takes
  time (assistant answers, appointment searches, notifications, nightly backups).
- **The app** ([`apple/`](../apple)) is a native SwiftUI app for iOS/iPadOS 26 and macOS 26. It
  has no data of its own: every screen reads and writes the server's API with the device's own
  token. Without a server it shows a read-only demo family.
- **The API** is open and documented ([API](/api)): personal API tokens let your own scripts and
  AI agents use it too.

## Modules you don't need

Every module except the calendar can be turned off per server, e.g.
`DISABLED_MODULES=appointments,finance`. It disappears from the app, its API answers 404, the
worker skips its jobs and the assistant loses its tools. The data stays; removing the setting
brings it back.

## Where it comes from

Ohana replaced three apps written for one family: a vacation calendar with finances and AI
(*family-planner*), a recipe and meal planner (*cookless*) and a doctor's appointment watcher
(*findmyappointment*). [Background and decisions](/plan) tells the story.
